Support

Questions, a bug or an idea? Email kalitaventures@gmail.com. A real person reads every message. You can also use Settings > Contact support in the app.

It helps to include your iPhone or iPad model, your iOS version and what you tapped just before the problem.

Common questions

How do I move my records to a new phone?

In Toot, open Settings > Backup and export > Export backup and save the file to Files, AirDrop it or email it to yourself. On the new device, open Toot, go to the same place and tap Import backup. Toot has no cloud sync, so this file is the way to move your data.

I bought Pro. How do I get it back?

Sign in with the same Apple Account, open Settings in Toot and tap Restore Purchases.

How do I cancel the Yearly plan?

Open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Toot. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date. For refunds, go to reportaproblem.apple.com.

My reminders don't arrive.

Check Settings > Notifications > Toot on your device and make sure notifications are allowed. Focus modes can also hold them back.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app. Your records exist only on your device, so they go with it. See the privacy policy for analytics and purchases.

Where does the advice in Toot come from?

From public guidance by the NHS, the American Dental Association and the World Health Organization. Each tip links to its source in the app (Settings > Sources). It is general information, not medical advice.
