Privacy Policy

Effective 25 October 2026

Toot keeps your dental records on your device. We don't have accounts, we don't sell data and we don't track you across apps or websites.

In short

  • Your teeth, notes, photos, documents, dentist details, appointments and name stay on your device. We never receive them.
  • There is no account and no iCloud or cloud sync. Backups are files you save yourself.
  • If you are 13 or older, Toot sends anonymous usage events to our own analytics service, Plumb. They never include health details.
  • If you tell Toot you are under 13, analytics never start on that device.
  • Purchases go through Apple. RevenueCat checks them so Pro works on your devices.

Who we are

Toot is made by Capymakes, which is operated by Rupangkan Kalita, the data controller for the data described here. Contact: kalitaventures@gmail.com.

Data that stays on your device

Everything you enter in Toot is stored only on your iPhone or iPad: brushing and floss sessions, streaks, tooth conditions and notes, smile photos, documents and scans, your dentist's contact details, appointments, reminder times, brush head dates and the first name you choose to give. We have no server that stores these, so we can't see, share or lose them.

Your name is optional. It is shown only inside the app and is not included in the PDF report.

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. The camera and photo library are used only when you add a smile photo or a document, and only after you allow access.

Backups and export

Toot does not sync to iCloud or any cloud service. When you tap Export backup, Toot creates a file and the share sheet lets you choose where it goes, for example Files, AirDrop or email. Once you share that file, it is handled by the place you sent it to. Import backup reads a file you pick. Your device's own backups (for example an iCloud Backup of the whole iPhone, if you have it turned on) are managed by Apple and your settings, as for any app.

Deleting your data

Deleting Toot from your device deletes all its records. Backup files you saved elsewhere stay where you put them.

Analytics (Plumb)

To learn which parts of Toot help people and where they get stuck, Toot sends usage events to Plumb, an analytics service we run ourselves (hosted on Vercel). Analytics only start after the age question in onboarding, and only if you are 13 or older.

What is sentWhat is never sent
Event names such as "onboarding step", "brushing session completed", "paywall shown"; counts and durations (for example how many documents, how many seconds); your onboarding choices as option codes (for example "twice a day"), and an age range only for people 13 and older; a random device ID and session ID created by Toot; the anonymous purchase ID from RevenueCat; app version, iOS version, device model, language and your device's region setting. Tooth conditions, tooth numbers, notes, photos, documents or their contents, dentist details, appointment details, your name, your email, contacts, precise location, advertising identifiers.

The country comes from your device's region setting. Plumb does not look up your location from your IP address. Events are not linked to your identity and are not used for advertising or tracking. We don't sell or share them.

Under 13: if you choose "Under 13" at the age question, Toot never starts analytics on that device and drops anything it was holding in memory. Toot is not directed at children under 13.

Purchases (Apple and RevenueCat)

Toot Pro is sold through the App Store. Apple handles payment; we never see your card or Apple Account details. Apple's privacy policy is at apple.com/legal/privacy.

We use RevenueCat to check purchases and restore Pro. RevenueCat receives your App Store purchase receipts, an anonymous app user ID and basic device information (such as app and iOS version and country). See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Links inside the app

Health tips in Toot cite public sources from the NHS, the American Dental Association and the World Health Organization. Tapping a source opens that page in Safari inside the app; those sites have their own privacy policies.

App Store privacy details

On the App Store, Toot lists these data types as collected, not linked to you and not used for tracking: Purchases (purchase history), Usage Data (product interaction), Identifiers (user ID and device ID) and Diagnostics (other diagnostic data). Toot collects no health, fitness, location, contact or financial information.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or US state privacy laws), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, or to object to its use. Because analytics events are anonymous and not linked to you, we usually can't match them to a person. Email us and we will help with any request we can act on. You can also stop analytics by deleting the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.
