Two minutes, twice a day.

Toot is a brushing timer and dental record for iPhone and iPad. Time each brush, keep your streak, log floss, note what your dentist did to each tooth and keep your appointments in one place.

Brushing timer

Four 30-second zones with a gentle tap when it's time to move on.

Streaks and floss

Your week at a glance, a floss log and a streak that counts mornings and nights.

Tooth map

Mark fillings, crowns and anything your dentist mentions, with your own notes.

Dentist

Contact, last cleaning, appointments with a reminder the day before, and your documents.

Your records stay on your phone

Toot has no account and no cloud sync. Your teeth, notes, photos and documents are stored on your device. You can save a backup file yourself whenever you like. Read the privacy policy for the details.

Free, with an optional Pro

The timer, streaks, floss log, tooth map, appointments, reminders, tips and backups are free. Toot Pro adds your full history, longer charts, unlimited smile photos and documents, and a PDF report for your dentist. Pro is a one-time Lifetime purchase or a Yearly subscription.

General information, not medical advice. Ask your dentist about your own teeth.
